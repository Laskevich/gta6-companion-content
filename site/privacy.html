Privacy Policy

App: Cheats for GTA 6 (unofficial guide). Last updated: November 21, 2026.

This app shows cheat codes, unlock guides and progress trackers for Grand Theft Auto VI. We built it to work without accounts and without collecting personal data.

What we do not collect

  • No account. The app never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, photos or other device data.

Data stored only on your device

Your challenge counters, your plan, your favorites, your controller choice, your alert settings and whether we already asked for a rating are saved on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you search on your iPhone, cheat and guide names may appear in Spotlight and Siri suggestions. This index is built and kept on your device by iOS.

Notifications

Alerts about new content and about GTA 6 Online are off until you turn them on. They are local notifications: the app checks the public content files in the background and shows the alert itself. No push service, no device token, nothing is sent to us.

Reports you send

The "Tell us" buttons open an email in your mail app with the cheat name, your console, the app and iOS version and the content date filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use your email address and message only to answer you and to fix the content, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

Content downloads

The app downloads cheat data from GitHub Pages (a service of GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when the files are requested. We do not receive or use these logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether you own the purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple, and does not receive your name or payment details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
