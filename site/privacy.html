Privacy Policy

App: Cheats for GTA 6 (unofficial guide). Last updated: November 20, 2026.

This app shows cheat codes, unlock guides and progress trackers for Grand Theft Auto VI. We built it to work without accounts and without collecting personal data.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email or phone number.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, photos or other device data.

Data stored only on your device

Your challenge counters, your plan, your controller choice and whether we already asked for a rating are saved on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Content downloads

The app downloads cheat data from GitHub Pages (a service of GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when the files are requested. We do not receive or use these logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether you own the purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple, and does not receive your name or payment details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
